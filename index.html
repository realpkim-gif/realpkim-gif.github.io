What I think about

/01

Honest evaluation

A model is only as good as the comparison behind it. I care about fair baselines, clean train/test splits, and reporting what didn't work — not just the best number.

/02

Ethics in ML

Models inherit the biases of their data. Working with messy real-world text showed me how easily preprocessing choices quietly change what a model learns.

/03

Technology and society

ML systems increasingly shape decisions people never see. I think the people building them should understand the consequences, not just the architecture.

/04

Learning in the open

I keep my projects public, including the early ones. Watching my own repos improve over time matters more to me than only showing polished work.
